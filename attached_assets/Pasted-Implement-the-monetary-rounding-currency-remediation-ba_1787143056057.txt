Implement the monetary rounding/currency remediation based on the completed
"Golden Life Currency and Monetary Rounding Audit".

IMPORTANT:
Do not mechanically replace Math.trunc(), Math.round(), Math.floor(), or toFixed().
Preserve precision during calculations and round only at defined monetary boundaries.

We do NOT need historical/backward compatibility for obsolete monetary fields or
legacy calculation paths. Use the current canonical architecture.

GOAL:
Establish ONE consistent monetary calculation and rounding policy across the
entire project.

1. CANONICAL ROUNDING

Use:
- shared/currency.ts as the currency policy
- server/lib/math.ts for backend monetary helpers
- roundBookingAmount() for booking currency
- round2()/roundToCents() for USD accounting

Zero-decimal currencies:
- HUF
- IRR
- JPY
- KRW

must produce integer final monetary values using proper half-up rounding.

USD/EUR/GBP and other two-decimal currencies must use two-decimal monetary
boundaries.

Do not round intermediate calculations unless required by an explicit
financial boundary.

2. PRICING / REVENUE ENGINE

Audit and fix:
- server/lib/pricing.ts
- server/lib/revenue-engine.ts

Do not feed display-rounded components back into later calculations.

Keep full precision internally.

Round only at the appropriate output boundaries:
- booking lines
- taxable subtotals
- taxes
- final patient total
- provider local snapshots
- final USD accounting amount

Ensure:

sum(lines) == final booking total

after applying the defined residual/reconciliation strategy.

3. TAX ENGINE

Fix server/lib/tax-engine.ts.

When service/platform taxable amounts are allocated proportionally:

- calculate using full precision
- round the final total
- allocate residual deterministically
- ensure:

service taxable amount + platform taxable amount
==
discounted taxable total

Likewise ensure:

service tax + platform tax == total tax

especially for HUF/IRR .5 cases.

Do not independently round buckets in a way that creates a residual mismatch.

4. PROVIDER SETTLEMENT

Fix server/lib/provider-settlement.ts.

Do not independently round every local component and then independently
convert every component to USD.

Instead:

local canonical aggregate
→ convert aggregate to USD
→ round USD accounting amount

Where USD component values are required, allocate residuals deterministically
so all components reconcile exactly with the canonical USD aggregate.

Ensure provider earnings, tax pass-through, commissions, platform deductions,
wallet settlement, and ledger values reconcile.

5. REFUNDS

Fix the confirmed currency mismatch in:

server/routes/admin/admin-financial.routes.ts

Do not write a USD refund amount into a booking-currency field.

Clearly distinguish:
- booking-currency refund snapshot
- USD accounting/payment refund amount

Trace the complete refund flow through:
- appointment
- payment
- wallet
- Stripe/payment provider
- provider settlement
- notifications
- reports

Ensure every value is interpreted in its actual currency.

6. FIXED / MIN / MAX / TRAVEL / PAYMENT FEES

Audit all fee rules in:

server/lib/revenue-engine.ts

Explicitly establish the currency contract for:
- fixed platform fees
- minimum platform fees
- maximum platform fees
- fixed payment adjustments
- travel fees
- percentage fees

Do not assume a fixed monetary rule is USD or booking currency without
confirming its intended contract from the existing implementation/schema.

Make the rule currency explicit where necessary.

7. CURRENCY CONVERSION

Follow this principle:

local booking calculation
→ final local aggregate
→ one USD conversion at accounting boundary

Avoid unnecessary:

local → USD → local

round trips.

Do not round during intermediate conversions.

Same-currency conversion should remain exact.

8. ZERO-DECIMAL VALIDATION

Enforce currency precision at monetary boundaries.

For HUF/IRR/JPY/KRW:
- booking totals must be integers
- tax must be integers
- discounts must be integers
- local refunds must be integers
- local payout amounts must be integers
- local monetary snapshots must not contain fractional values

Do not blindly change database column types unless required by the existing
schema architecture. Enforce the correct monetary value at write boundaries.
