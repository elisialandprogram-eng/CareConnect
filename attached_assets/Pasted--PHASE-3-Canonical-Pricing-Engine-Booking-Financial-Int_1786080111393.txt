# PHASE 3 — Canonical Pricing Engine & Booking Financial Integrity

Phases 1 and 2 established the canonical financial architecture and security model.

This phase establishes a **single canonical Pricing Engine** and an **immutable Booking Pricing Snapshot**.

The objective is to guarantee that every price displayed anywhere in the system is identical and originates from one calculation.

---

# Primary Objective

There must be exactly **ONE Pricing Engine**.

Every consumer of booking pricing must use the same immutable pricing snapshot.

No component, service, email, notification, invoice, report, or dashboard may independently calculate booking prices, taxes, fees, discounts, or provider earnings.

---

# 1. Implement a Canonical Pricing Engine

Create one authoritative Pricing Engine responsible for all booking financial calculations.

The Pricing Engine must calculate:

* Base service price
* Service duration adjustments
* Provider pricing
* Country pricing
* Currency
* Membership discounts
* Coupons
* Promotional discounts
* Travel fees
* Emergency fees
* Platform fees
* Taxes
* Provider earnings
* Platform earnings
* Final payable amount

No other service may calculate these values independently.

---

# 2. Create an Immutable Booking Pricing Snapshot

When the user confirms a booking, calculate the pricing once and persist a complete pricing snapshot with the booking.

The snapshot becomes the financial contract for that booking.

It must contain every monetary value required by downstream systems.

After the booking is created:

* do not recalculate pricing
* do not recalculate taxes
* do not recalculate discounts
* do not recalculate provider earnings

Every downstream system must read the stored snapshot.

---

# 3. Eliminate Duplicate Price Calculations

Review the complete booking flow.

Remove duplicate pricing logic from:

* booking preview
* booking confirmation
* payment creation
* appointment details
* client dashboard
* provider dashboard
* admin dashboard
* invoices
* receipts
* email templates
* SMS
* WhatsApp
* push notifications
* booking history
* financial reports
* exports
* provider earnings
* wallet
* ledger
* refunds
* cancellations

All consumers must use the canonical pricing snapshot.

---

# 4. Canonical Tax Engine

Implement one centralized Tax Engine.

Taxes must be calculated exactly once during pricing.

Persist the calculated tax in the pricing snapshot.

Invoices, emails, dashboards, exports, reports, refunds, and settlements must use the stored tax value.

No downstream tax recalculation is permitted.

---

Only the Pricing Engine performs calculations.

Every subsequent step reads the stored snapshot.

---

# 6. Currency Consistency

Ensure every monetary value within a booking uses the same currency context.

Validate:

* booking price
* taxes
* discounts
* provider earnings
* wallet
* ledger
* invoices
* emails
* notifications

No mixed-currency calculations.

No hidden conversions after booking confirmation.

---

# 8. Refund & Cancellation Integrity

Refunds and cancellations must reference the original pricing snapshot.

Do not recalculate prices during refunds.

Refund calculations must always use the stored booking values.

---

# 9. Versioning

Support pricing versioning.

Each booking should record:

* pricing engine version
* tax engine version
* pricing calculation timestamp

Future pricing rule changes must not alter historical bookings.
