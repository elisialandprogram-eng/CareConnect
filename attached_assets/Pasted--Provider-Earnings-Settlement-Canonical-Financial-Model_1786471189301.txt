# Provider Earnings & Settlement — Canonical Financial Model Cleanup

Implement this completely across the project. **Do not perform an audit-only exercise. Inspect the existing implementation, identify every affected path, and immediately fix every issue found.**

The project is not launched yet, so prioritize correctness and architectural consistency over backward compatibility with obsolete internal implementations.

## PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Create **one canonical provider earnings and settlement model** used everywhere.

There must be:

* One canonical source of truth for provider earnings.
* One canonical calculation for provider net earnings.
* One canonical settlement flow.
* No legacy provider-earning calculation.
* No duplicated provider earnings formulas.
* No UI calculating provider earnings independently.
* No platform tax being treated as provider earnings.
* No stale `providerEarning`-style fields being used as an alternative source of truth.
* No contradictory earnings values between dashboard, earnings history, appointment details, notifications, emails, reports, exports, payout screens, or APIs.

Do not simply rename legacy fields or wrap old calculations. **Remove obsolete financial paths when they are no longer required.**

---

# 1. ESTABLISH THE CANONICAL FINANCIAL OWNERSHIP MODEL

The financial model must clearly separate the:

### Service domain

Owned economically by the provider:

* Service/base price
* Home-visit/service-related fees
* Clinic/service-related fees
* Online/service-related fees
* Emergency/service-related fees
* Surge/service-related fees
* Applicable service/travel charges
* Service tax

### Platform domain

Owned economically by the platform:

* Platform fee
* Platform tax
* Payment-method/platform charges
* Platform-side administrative charges
* Other explicitly platform-owned charges

### Provider deductions

Only charges explicitly defined as provider-borne may reduce provider earnings, such as:

* Platform commission
* Explicit provider-side payment/settlement charges
* Other explicitly configured provider deductions

Do not infer ownership from the name of a field. Trace the actual business meaning.

---

# 2. CANONICAL PROVIDER EARNINGS FORMULA

Provider earnings must follow one canonical formula.

For a normal service booking:

```text
Provider Net Earnings =
Service Price
+ Service-Related Fees
+ Service Tax
- Provider-Borne Discounts/Adjustments
- Platform Commission
- Other Explicit Provider-Borne Charges
```

### CRITICAL TAX RULE

If tax is applied to the service:

```text
Service tax → provider
```

If tax is applied to the platform fee:

```text
Platform tax → platform
```

**Platform tax must NEVER be included in provider earnings.**

For example:

```text
Service price       1,000
Service tax 27%       270

Platform fee           50
Platform tax 27%       13.50

Commission 4%          40
```

Provider earnings:

```text
1,000
+ 270
- 40
----
1,230 HUF
```

NOT:

```text
1,000 + 270 + 13.50 - 40
```

The `13.50` platform tax belongs entirely to the platform domain.

---

# 3. PATIENT TOTAL MUST REMAIN SEPARATE

Patient total is a different financial concept.

Example:

```text
Service price          1,000
Service tax              270
Platform fee              50
Platform tax               13.50
--------------------------------
Patient paid            1,333.50
```

The system must never assume:

```text
Patient paid = Provider earnings
```

These are separate concepts.

Canonical relationship:

```text
Patient Payment
        │
        ├── Provider service economics
        │      ├── Service price
        │      ├── Service fees
        │      └── Service tax
        │
        └── Platform economics
               ├── Platform fee
               └── Platform tax
```

# 4. ONLINE PAYMENT PROVIDER UI

For Stripe, wallet, and other non-cash payment methods, the provider does NOT need the full internal financial calculation.

Provider-facing display should primarily show:

```text
Patient paid        1,333.50 HUF

Your net earnings   1,230.00 HUF
```

The provider should not be forced to understand:

* Platform tax
* Platform taxable subtotal
* Internal settlement movements
* Tax-engine version
* Pricing-engine version
* Internal USD conversion
* Payment gateway accounting
* Internal ledger movements
* Reconciliation values
* Platform revenue calculations

Those remain internal/admin/accounting data.

The provider can optionally see the service-level earnings breakdown if useful, but do not expose unnecessary internal settlement complexity.

---

# 5. CASH BOOKING PROVIDER UI

Cash bookings are different because the provider physically collects money from the patient.

For cash bookings, provider-facing information must clearly distinguish:

### Patient payment

```text
Patient paid
```

### Provider earnings

```text
Service price
+ Service tax
- Platform commission
= Your net earnings
```

### Cash settlement

If applicable:

```text
Cash collected
Platform fee
Platform tax
Other cash/remittance amount
Amount to remit/settle
```

The exact cash settlement formula must follow the existing canonical settlement business rules.

Do NOT put platform tax into provider earnings merely because the provider collected that tax in cash.

The provider is collecting certain platform-owned amounts on behalf of the platform; collection does not make those amounts provider earnings.

# 9. TAX OWNERSHIP MUST FLOW FROM THE TAX ENGINE

The canonical tax engine already separates:

```text
Service tax
Platform tax
```

Preserve that separation all the way through:

```text
Tax Engine
    ↓
Pricing / Revenue Engine
    ↓
Appointment Snapshot
    ↓
Settlement Engine
    ↓
Provider Earnings
    ↓
Platform Economics
```

Do not recombine the two taxes into one provider payout number.

The provider earnings calculation must consume:

```text
serviceTaxAmount
```

when applicable.

It must NOT consume:

```text

```

if `totalTax` includes platform tax.

# 10. TAX DISPLAY

Do not display combined rates such as:

```text
Tax (32%)
```

when service tax is 27% and platform tax is 5%.

Use:

```text
Service tax (27%)       33.75
Platform tax (5%)        0.50
```

or simply:

```text
Service tax              33.75
Platform tax               0.50
```

The rate must correspond to the taxable base it actually applies to.

Never calculate a fake combined percentage by:

```text
serviceRate + platformRate
```
