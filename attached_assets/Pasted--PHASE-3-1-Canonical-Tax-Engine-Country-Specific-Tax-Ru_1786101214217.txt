# PHASE 3.1 — Canonical Tax Engine & Country-Specific Tax Rules

Phase 3 established the canonical Pricing Engine and immutable Booking Pricing Snapshot.

This phase establishes the **canonical Tax Engine**.

The objective is to ensure there is **one authoritative tax calculation architecture** across the platform and eliminate every remaining tax inconsistency.

---

# Primary Objectives

Implement a single canonical Tax Engine.

There must be exactly one place where taxes are calculated.

All bookings, packages, memberships, invoices, refunds, notifications, provider earnings, reports, and financial records must derive their tax information from the canonical Tax Engine.

No route, controller, email, invoice, report, or service may calculate tax independently.

---

# 1. Country-Specific Sub-Service Tax Rules

Replace the legacy single `sub_services.tax_percentage` approach with country-specific tax rules.

Implement a new canonical data model.

Example:

sub_service_tax_rules

* id
* sub_service_id
* country_code
* tax_rate
* effective_from
* effective_to
* is_active
* created_at
* updated_at

Every sub-service may have different tax rates for different countries.

Example:

Physiotherapy

Hungary → 27%

Iran → 9%

UAE → 5%

UK → 0%

The legacy `sub_services.tax_percentage` should become a compatibility field only during migration.

The new table becomes the single authoritative source for service tax.

---

# 2. Separate Service Tax from Platform Tax

The platform currently mixes service taxation with platform taxation.

Implement two independent tax domains.

## Service Tax

Applies to provider-delivered services.

Taxable components include:

* base service price
* home visit fee
* distance/travel fee
* emergency fee
* provider service add-ons
* other provider-related service charges

Does NOT include:

* platform fee
* payment gateway fee
* admin fee
* convenience fee

Service tax must always come from the country-specific sub-service tax rule.

---

## Platform Tax

Applies only to platform-generated charges.

Taxable components include:

* platform fee
* payment gateway fee
* admin fee
* convenience fee

Platform tax comes from the global country tax configuration.

Platform tax must never tax provider service charges.

---

# 3. Canonical Tax Engine

The Tax Engine becomes responsible for:

* resolving country-specific service tax
* resolving platform tax
* calculating taxable subtotals
* calculating service tax
* calculating platform tax
* calculating total tax
* returning a complete immutable tax result

No other service may calculate tax.

---

# 4. Pricing Engine Integration

The Pricing Engine must delegate all tax calculations to the Tax Engine.

The Pricing Engine must never perform tax calculations directly.

The Tax Engine returns a complete tax result that is included in the Booking Pricing Snapshot.

---

# 5. Booking Pricing Snapshot

Extend the immutable pricing snapshot.

Store:

* service subtotal
* service tax rate
* service tax amount
* platform fee
* payment gateway fee
* admin fee
* platform tax rate
* platform tax amount
* total tax
* total amount
* pricing version
* tax version
* calculation timestamp

Historical bookings must remain immutable.

Future tax changes must never alter historical bookings.

---

# 6. Tax Rule Resolution

Implement deterministic tax resolution.

Service tax:

1. Find an active country-specific tax rule for the selected sub-service.
2. Apply that rule to service-related charges.
3. If no rule exists, service tax is 0%.
4. Log a warning for missing configuration.

Platform tax:

1. Resolve the active country platform tax.
2. Apply only to platform-related charges.
3. If no rule exists, apply 0%.
4. Log a warning.

Never substitute one country's tax rule for another.

---

# 7. Remove Legacy Tax Logic

Find and remove every direct tax calculation.

Examples include:

price × taxRate

subtotal × taxRate

priceWithTax

Any inline tax formulas.

Every tax calculation must call the Tax Engine.

Remove duplicate tax calculations from:

* appointment booking
* package purchases
* memberships
* invoices
* receipts
* refunds
* cancellations
* reports
* exports
* dashboards
* notifications
* emails
* provider earnings
* reconciliation

The package purchase flow must use the canonical Tax Engine instead of calculating tax directly.

---

# 8. Admin Management

Provide a complete administrative interface.

Administrators should be able to:

* manage country-specific service tax rules
* activate/deactivate rules
* define effective dates
* view historical versions
* manage global platform tax rules
* preview tax calculations

Tax rules must be configuration-driven.

Adding a new country must not require code changes.

---

# 9. Migration

Safely migrate the existing system.

Migration requirements:

* preserve existing bookings
* preserve historical invoices
* preserve historical tax amounts
* migrate legacy tax percentages into the new tax rule table
* do not modify historical pricing snapshots

Legacy bookings should continue using their stored tax values.

Only new bookings should use the canonical tax rules after migration.
